# Privacy Policy — Mahjong with Friends

**Effective date:** 2026-08-17

This is the whole policy. It is short because the app collects almost nothing.

## What is stored

- **A display name you choose.** Typed by you, shown to the other players at
  your table. Stored on your own device, and held in the game server's memory
  only while you are in a room.
- **A random device identifier.** Generated on your device the first time the
  app opens. It is a random string with no connection to your name, phone
  number, email, advertising ID, or any account. Its only job is to give your
  seat back to you if your connection drops mid-game.
- **Gameplay traffic while you are playing** — the tiles you draw and discard,
  and the score of the hand. This lives in the game server's memory for the
  duration of the room and is gone when the room closes.

## What is NOT collected

- No accounts, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords.
- No advertising identifiers, and **no ads of any kind**.
- No analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs that collect data.
- No location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or files.
- No payment information. Nothing in the app is purchasable.
- Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

## Retention

Nothing is kept. There is no database. Game state lives in server memory and is
destroyed when the room closes or the server restarts. Your display name and
device identifier live on your own device; deleting the app deletes them.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal
information, so there is nothing to obtain parental consent for.

## Security

Traffic between the app and the game server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS and
secure WebSockets).

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the effective date above changes with it. Material
changes will be noted in the app's store listing.
